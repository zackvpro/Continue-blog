# 笔记

### 前言
国家能力体现为国家征集税收、提供司法、秩序和社会公共产品，以及实现国家政策目标的能力。
国家能力理论与对中国特色的国家治理机制的研究间的结合有限，这带来学术研究的视角缺憾。
例如，国家能力理论研究经常将国家作为单一的行动者，但中国的事权高度集中于地方政府，而国家正式预算之外的土地财政收入占地方政府以上。因此，央地关系、地方政府激励等视角是探讨国家能力理论时不可或缺的。([[中国科层制的动员属性；运动式治理：特色动员机制]])一旦笼统地探讨，变很难描述同一国家体制下，不同的治理领域中，国家能力呈现强弱并存的局面，也无法描述国家能力的动态演进过程。
反过来，以委托代理关系作为国家治理机制的理论分析也易陷入解释上的困境。“策略主义”“运动式治理”“一统体制与有效治理的冲突”等理论在特定领域有效，但却很难给出改善中国国家治理模式的出路。中国在相对短的历史中，许多领域取得了历史罕见的成就，官僚制（科层制）的理性化、制度化、法治化却并不构成主要驱动因素。这使得对方案的研究透露苍白。而这些方案的困境的一大原因就是忽略了国家基础能力的积累与进步。委托代理机制与基础能力的互动最终决定了相关领域的国家能力和治理绩效：**基础能力的演进受国家代理人建设基础能力的意愿和动机的影响，基础能力的变化反过来进一步影响代理人的激励和行为。**
作者选取“行政发包制”作为委托代理行政制度分析的代表主要有两个原因：
1. 行政发包制以属地化主机行政发包为特征，构成中国长期稳态的国家治理形式；
2. 行政发包制所强调的“行政权分配、预算分配与经济激励、内部控制与绩效考核”与国家能力理论的“决策自主性、资源汲取动员以及政策执行”相对应
选取“基础性权力”作为国家能力的代表，反映的是发包方和承包方的专业化水平、资金预算、人员规模以及治理技术等方面的“基础设施”水平和攻击能力，与强调信息不对称、代理人激励和策略反应的“治理机制”形成对应。如此一来，国家能力与代理人的互动关系，简化为基础性权力与行政发包制间的关系。

### 西方理论中的国家能力
为了研究欧洲绝对专制主义国家之间的战争如何迫使君主中央权力机构渗透社会，大规模征税和汲取社会资源、发展军事动员能力，“国家能力”的概念被提出。在这个过程中，分散于各地贵族或自治社会的行政、司法和军事权力被逐渐集中，国家提供安全秩序和公共服务、实现国家目标的能力愈发强大。
而这种国家能力，以“专制性权力”与“基础性权力”区分开。“专制性权力”是指国家不与公民社会团体协商而单独行动的能力，”基础性权力“是指国家渗透社会，实现其政策法令的“后勤”保障能力，主要与国家的“精细管理”直接相关。
“强国家、弱社会”与“弱国家、强社会”概念（Migdal, 1988）：当地方强人掌握着地方社会的经济、文化资源进而实施强力控制时，国家政策很难渗透至社会，政策法令也难以令行禁止。即，有限的国家权力资源、强大的地方势力和社会网络限制了国家权力的渗透。
基于“发展型国家”概念发展的“嵌入性自主”，强调国家能力的两个关键维度：一是国家对于社会团体的“嵌入性”，国家机构与社会的密切互动关系为资源汲取和政策的渗透提供重要的保障；二是国家的自主性，即嵌入社会之中的国家机器不被社会力量捕获和寻租，能够自主制定和贯彻国家目标。
西方理论聚焦的国家能力理论是从法理型科层制（韦伯式科层制）的演化过程中总结而来的。尤其是，政治与行政的分离（国家意志的表达与执行的分离）彻底塑造了官僚机构的独立性和专业性特征。韦伯式科层/0制支持了政府的政策制定与官僚的严格执行力，有限政府（依法行政与规则程序至上），国家与公民社会的严格边界（杜绝人情关系和非正式关系网络介入）。
国家作为单一行动者，大致符合西方国家和政治机构集中化和全国化的事实。也因此与中国的央地互动格局存在理论差异。

### 国家能力视角与行政发包制
行政发包制是在一个权威体制的上下级之间引入的发包-承包关系，在三个维度上与国家能力的核心维度形成对应。
从行政权的分配上看，发包方掌握了所有名义上的权威（监督权、否决权、指导权），承包方所拥有的是实际控制权与自由裁量权，两者的权力转移无明显的授权过程。这与科层制设计旨在消灭代理人的自由裁量权，而代理人也以程序的名义保护自己。
“专制性权力”能否转化为“基础性权力”，存在一个发包方与承包方的对立关系，主要为上级政府与下级政府、地方政府与行政外包方（企业和非盈利组织等）。
国家能力所强调的自主性，体现在发包方分派目标任务的权力。”嵌入型自主“体现在地方官员与企业领导者的密切关系，使两者保持了较好的沟通。

>[!WARNING] 笔者注
>”基础性权力“的概念有误用的情况。将下级政府与行政外包方作为”基础性权力“的掌握者，实质是将地方拥有的事权或是实际执行权认作”基础性权力“，与原意有出入。

从经费自筹和经济激励上看，行政发包制存在发包方的集中性汲取和预算分配，但更重要的是承包方的经费自筹和财力分摊。在改革开放时代，地方政府预算外财政、城投公司等融资机制成为地方政府财力的关键组成部分。这与西方视角下的全国性征税机构有显著差异。
从内部控制和监督考核方面，行政发包制中的人格化问责与结果导向考核是国家政策执行的重要机制。相较于以程序为主导的韦伯式科层制，中国科层制依靠纵向监督和人格化的绩效考核，以行政区划边界作为责任边界的绩效考核方式，”谁主管、谁负责“。人格化问责赋予承包方激励和压力，助于撬动承包方利用各种非正式手段实现正式目标。

### 政治激励、基础能力与行政发包制的谱系

区分行政发包制所对应的国家能力强弱的两个方向为：承包方在特定承包事项上所面临的激励强度；“基础能力”作为分析维度。
行政权分配、预算分配与经济激励、绩效考核与问责作为行政发包制的三个维度，对应着“分权激励、经济激励和政治激励”，共同构成特定治理领域的激励特征。地方政府面对多任务、多目标的治理格局，受地方领导人注意力和治理资源的双重约束，因而在治理目标的权衡间，绩效考核结构影响关键。其中最重要的是政治激励制度，在作者2014年的聚焦于晋升锦标赛中行政发包制的互动关系中的文章中，指出“晋升激励强、行政发包强”的领域为强项，“晋升激励弱、行政发包强”的领域为弱项。“晋升激励弱、行政发包弱”的国家级工程项目领域同样为强项。但目前还没有符合“晋升激励强，行政发包弱”的领域。

>[!WARNING] 笔者注
>晋升激励与行政发包关系这一部分问题比较突出：相较于两种制度的互动关系，更大程度是晋升激励的影响。原因有二：不存在“晋升激励强，行政发包弱”的领域，意味着晋升激励在整体制度影响更为关键；“晋升激励弱，行政发包弱”的领域主要是由于负责部门由地方转向中央，很难说明问题。

以“基础能力”作为分析维度，基础能力指发包方或承包方可以依赖的治理技术、专业技术、资源汲取和动员能力以及协调整合能力的总和水平。笔者说：

> 它在一定程度上决定了发包方从“专制性权力”到“基础性权力”的传递效果及最终实现的组织绩效。

基础能力的高低直接影响到行政发包制的结构参数和运行效率。例如，技术变革带来更强的资源汲取能力，提高承包方的职业化水平等。（只有承包方吗？）
至此，承包方激励的强弱与基础能力的强弱构成互动关系。治理领域的成功失败，取决于承包方在特定治理事项上所具有的激励特征与基础能力的组合情况。

|基础能力\承包方激励 |强     |   弱   |
| ----            | --- |----|  
|  强   | 招商引资、计划生育、精准扶贫等    | 日常化的跨区域、跨部门合作     |
|弱     | 各类专项整治、欠发达地区普及教育  |帝制时代的大众教育、19世纪末的环境保护 |

在这种状态下，基础能力几乎决定了治理领域的成败。晋升激励机制更多决定了短期内的注意力分配情况。但在基础能力满足条件时，面对地方政府与社会的互动时，仍需要通过晋升激励机制落实责任。

该文章粗读时颇觉惊叹，细读时却不然。科层制与国家治理的关系被矮化，颇有“基础能力”决定治理能效的态势。”基础能力“，这里主要指官僚间进行委托代理时的信息互动效率，提升的过程被简单地归类为信息的获取、整理与确认的技术的提高过程，这种提高过程使得激励目标更加清晰，进而通过委托代理关系实现满意的结果。但”基础性能力“问题并非信息技术问题，而是央地互动的问题，信息技术所构成的”基础能力“只是”基础性能力“的一个构成要素。毕竟韦伯式科层制的欧美国家在信息技术尚无今日发达时，便已建立起足够国家运行的”基础性能力“。没有这种充足的”基础性能力“既是历史沿革问题、也是高层的政治策略问题。政治发包制的中国式科层制恰恰取代了这种韦伯式科层制的生态位，在无明显政治动乱时，中国式科层制将是长期的。尽管笔者对要求用韦伯式科层制取代中国式科层制的批评不敢兴趣，也希望对中国式科层制进行进一步的改革，但曲解国家能力理论以逢合中国的现状这点笔者也不赞同。
政治发包制的三个维度”行政权分配、预算分配、绩效考核“与国家能力理论的“决策自主性、资源汲取与动员、政策执行”的对应不明显、逻辑刻意。或者说政治制度必然要面临“行政权力的分配问题、行政资源的来源问题、行政目标的执行问题”，政治发包制的三个维度只是在回答这个问题而已。被引入的”基础能力“维度则被用以掩饰政治发包制中的扭曲的激励机制问题与扭曲的权责对等问题。
笔者认为，对中国科层制的问题的研究终究要回答”激励机制“与”权责对等“这两个问题。文中大提”河长制“的治理成效，是片面的，将环境治理阶段的”补课“作为大幅进步是有问题，环境治理这类”对数函数性“的问题均一开始成效显著、后面就困难，前面的阶段的经验参考有限。例如扶贫，短期突击的成效显著，但扶贫是长期主义的问题，不返贫的难度比扶贫高很多。找未来的方向要找”指数函数性“的经验，即前面铺展时困难重重，但最后效果是长期的，比如分税制改革的经验。所谓改革进入”深水区“，便是指现在的问题是核心的，是直指中央的。
全国性的事务、外溢范围强的公共品需要由中央承担，不能再压给地方，中央不能挑轻的担子，却有着大量的行李。中央要扩张，地方要退缩，这是未来中国式科层制的重要方向。同样地，中央事务的扩张也将面临注意力机制的问题，与此伴随的中央政治制度改革也是呼之欲出的。

# Analytical Evaluation of the "Administrative Contracting System": A Theoretical and Practical Critique of State Capacity Paradigms in Chinese Governance

## Introduction: The Contested Paradigms of State Capacity

The discourse surrounding Chinese state capacity and its unique bureaucratic mechanisms has long been dominated by attempts to reconcile Western theoretical frameworks with the historical and political realities of China's governance structures. A prominent framework in this ongoing academic endeavor is the "Administrative Contracting System" (ACS), extensively theorized by scholars such as Zhou Li-An. This framework posits that the Chinese state operates as a hybrid between a Weberian bureaucracy and a corporate subcontracting model, where the central government acts as a principal delegating vast economic, social, and political responsibilities to local government agents. While this theory has provided significant explanatory leverage for China's rapid economic ascent—particularly when coupled with the dynamics of political promotion tournaments —it has also invited rigorous critical scrutiny.

The blog post under review provides a penetrating, deeply analytical critique of the ACS framework, specifically challenging its 2022 iteration which attempts to map administrative contracting onto the broader theoretical literature of state capacity. The reviewer's core thesis is that the ACS framework fundamentally misapplies established Western concepts of state capacity—namely Michael Mann’s distinction between "despotic" and "infrastructural" power—to mask structural deficiencies in China's central-local governance model. Furthermore, the reviewer argues that the concept of "basic capacity" is technologically reductionist, ignoring the deeply political nature of institutional power. Finally, utilizing a highly original mathematical metaphor comparing logarithmic and exponential policy outcomes, the reviewer asserts that the current bureaucratic incentive structures are optimized for short-term, campaign-style successes rather than the long-term, compounding institutional reforms required for China's future stability.

This report provides an exhaustive, multi-dimensional evaluation of the reviewer's critique. By synthesizing empirical data across various policy domains—ranging from pandemic control and food security to environmental governance and real estate—and contextualizing these within historical and sociological frameworks, this analysis aims to unpack the epistemological and practical limitations of the Administrative Contracting System.

## The Epistemological Misappropriation of Infrastructural Power

The foundational critique raised by the reviewer centers on the misapplication of Michael Mann’s widely cited typology of state power. Mann (1984) famously bifurcated state power into two distinct categories: "despotic power" and "infrastructural power". Despotic power refers to the range of actions that a state elite can undertake without routine, institutionalized negotiation with civil society groups. It is the power of the central authority to act autonomously and, if necessary, coercively. Infrastructural power, conversely, is defined as the logistical capacity of the state to penetrate civil society and seamlessly implement political decisions throughout the realm. This conceptualization has been highly influential in comparative politics, used to explain variations in state effectiveness from Latin America to East Asia.

In the ACS framework, the dynamic between the principal (the central government or higher-level authority) and the agent (local governments or third-party contractors) is structurally mapped onto these concepts. The central government's ability to assign mandates and veto outcomes is equated with despotic power, while the local government's actual control over resources, execution, and discretionary implementation is framed as infrastructural power.

The reviewer astutely identifies this as a critical conceptual error. Infrastructural power, in its original sociological conception, describes the relationship between the _state and society_, not the relationship between _different tiers of the state apparatus_. When a county-level government executes a directive from Beijing, the local government is not acting as the "infrastructure" of society; it is merely a subordinate node in the state hierarchy exercising delegated authority.

### Table 1: Theoretical Divergence in State Power Conceptualization

|**Analytical Dimension**|**Michael Mann’s Original Framework**|**The Administrative Contracting Framework**|**The Reviewer's Critique**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Core Locus of Power**|The nexus between state institutions and civil society.|The hierarchical relationship between upper and lower government tiers.|Conflates intra-bureaucratic delegation with true state-society penetration.|
|**Despotic Power**|The state's ability to act without societal negotiation.|The central authority's power to assign tasks, supervise, and veto.|Central control over local agents is administrative hierarchy, not despotic power over society.|
|**Infrastructural Power**|Logistical penetration of society (taxation, policing, services).|Local governments' discretionary execution and resource mobilization.|Misidentifies local administrative execution rights (事权) as infrastructural power.|

By relabeling local administrative execution as "infrastructural power," the ACS framework obfuscates the reality of Joel Migdal's "strong society, weak state" paradigm. As the reviewer notes, true infrastructural power requires a unified, professionalized apparatus deeply embedded within the population, acting upon institutionalized rules. When the central state subcontracts its obligations to local agents, it often relies on the agents' informal networks, personalistic ties, and coercive tactics to achieve targets, which is inherently contrary to the rationalized, rule-bound penetration characteristic of high infrastructural power in Weberian states.

## The Technological Reductionism of "Basic Capacity"

The second major pillar of the reviewer's critique targets the concept of "basic capacity" (基础能力) as utilized in the ACS framework. Within the contracting model, basic capacity is presented as the aggregate of governance technologies, professional expertise, and resource mobilization capabilities available to the contracting parties. It is framed as the crucial variable that determines whether despotic authority can be successfully translated into infrastructural outcomes.

The reviewer challenges this by arguing that the ACS framework reduces the evolution of state capacity to a mere advancement in information technology and data processing. The implicit logic of the ACS framework is that if information asymmetries between the central and local governments can be resolved through better monitoring technologies, the principal-agent problem will be mitigated, leading to optimal governance outcomes.

This technological determinism fundamentally ignores the historical realities of state formation. As the reviewer correctly asserts, Western European nations established robust, Weberian bureaucratic systems and formidable infrastructural power long before the advent of modern information technology. The success of these early modern states was not predicated on digital surveillance or real-time data transmission, but rather on political and institutional choices: the strict separation of politics and administration, the establishment of impersonal rule of law, and the centralization of fiscal revenues.

The reviewer argues that "basic capacity" is not an issue of information technology, but rather a profound question of central-local political dynamics. The failure to achieve high infrastructural power in certain domains is not due to a lack of data, but rather a deliberate political strategy—the "logic of empire" —whereby the central state intentionally maintains a flexible, ambiguous, and decentralized administrative structure to minimize its direct governance costs and deflect localized discontent.

This historical continuity is evident when examining the pre-imperial origins of Chinese statecraft. Data on the creation of counties during the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods reveals that early state formation relied heavily on military mobilization and the direct extraction of resources by noble clans rather than a centralized, unified logistical bureaucracy. The modern iteration of administrative subcontracting replaces noble fiefdoms with municipal and county governments, yet the underlying logic remains: the center demands outcomes but shifts the logistical and financial burden downward. Therefore, substituting genuine political institutionalization with "governance technology" serves to rationalize the enduring vulnerabilities of the Chinese bureaucratic model rather than providing a roadmap for overcoming them.

## The Mechanics and Distortions of the Contracting System

To fully appreciate the depth of the reviewer's critique, it is necessary to examine the three functional dimensions of the Administrative Contracting System: the distribution of administrative authority, the allocation of budgets, and the mechanisms of performance evaluation. The reviewer argues that aligning these three dimensions with the core tenets of state capacity (decision autonomy, resource mobilization, and policy execution) is a forced and artificial mapping.

### Authority Distribution and the Illusion of Autonomy

In a pure Weberian bureaucracy, agents are bound by strict procedural rules designed to eliminate arbitrary discretion. In the ACS, the central authority retains nominal control, but immense discretionary power is transferred to local officials without formal legal authorization. This structural ambiguity forces local officials to rely on informal, often extralegal, mechanisms to fulfill mandates. The autonomy celebrated by the ACS is, in reality, a survival mechanism for local agents operating under intense top-down pressure, rather than genuine "embedded autonomy" that characterizes developmental states.

### Budget Allocation and Fiscal Federalism

Perhaps the most critical distortion identified by the reviewer is in the realm of resource extraction. The ACS is characterized by the central government's centralized extraction of revenue coupled with the localized burden of expenditure. Unlike Western states where national tax agencies directly fund national initiatives, Chinese local governments are forced to self-finance central mandates.

This dynamic has profound macroeconomic consequences. It is the primary driver behind China's reliance on land-based finance and the proliferation of Local Government Financing Vehicles (LGFVs). Because local governments lack the formal fiscal capacity to meet their contracted targets, they commodify land and leverage debt, creating systemic financial risks in the real estate market. The reviewer rightly points out that framing this desperate pursuit of extrabudgetary revenue as a form of "resource mobilization capacity" is highly problematic. It masks a deeply dysfunctional fiscal arrangement under the guise of entrepreneurial governance.

### Performance Evaluation and Mismatched Incentives

The third dimension of the ACS involves results-oriented, highly personalized performance evaluations, commonly described as political tournaments. Local officials are evaluated not on their adherence to the rule of law, but on their ability to meet quantifiable targets, such as GDP growth.

The empirical literature strongly supports the reviewer's assertion that this leads to severe incentive misalignments. For example, in the realm of agricultural policy, the Food Security Responsibility (FSR) system transfers the burden of national food security to local governments. Because local officials are primarily incentivized by GDP growth and promotion, they view agricultural production as a high-cost, low-return endeavor. Consequently, they strategically adjust their behavior, intensifying unsustainable practices like over-irrigation to meet short-term grain quotas at the expense of long-term ecological stability. Similarly, during the COVID-19 pandemic, variations in local lockdown policies demonstrated that subnational officials prioritize career prospects tied to Beijing's targets, often resulting in either excessive compliance (draconian lockdowns) or selective implementation depending on local capacity constraints, reflecting a system driven by political intent rather than objective infrastructural capacity.

## Campaign-Style Governance vs. Sustainable Institutionalization

The blog reviewer takes particular issue with the ACS framework's reliance on "political tournaments" as the ultimate engine of state capacity. The ACS literature posits that the combination of strong promotion incentives and strong administrative contracting results in highly effective governance. Conversely, areas with weak promotion incentives and weak contracting are deemed inefficient.

The reviewer challenges the validity of this matrix. They observe that domains characterized by "weak promotion, weak contracting"—such as national aerospace programs or high-speed rail development—are often highly successful precisely because they are removed from the local contracting system and managed directly by the central state. This observation deals a significant blow to the ACS paradigm. It suggests that when the state requires genuine, uncompromising infrastructural power, it bypasses the administrative contracting system entirely and reverts to direct, centralized bureaucratic management.

Furthermore, the reviewer highlights the limitations of "campaign-style governance" (运动式治理), which is the natural byproduct of the ACS. When local governments face intense, periodic pressure from above to solve a specific problem (e.g., targeted poverty alleviation or environmental crackdowns), they mobilize all available formal and informal resources in a sudden burst of activity. While this can yield spectacular short-term results, it fails to build the durable, rule-based institutions necessary for long-term governance.

The implementation of the "River Chief" system, frequently cited as a triumph of local policy innovation in environmental governance , is criticized by the reviewer as merely "making up for lost time" (补课). Treating rapid environmental remediation as a breakthrough in state capacity ignores the fact that the environmental degradation was caused by the exact same administrative contracting system prioritizing GDP over ecology in previous decades.

## The Mathematics of Governance: Logarithmic vs. Exponential Paradigms

The most profound and conceptually innovative aspect of the reviewer's critique is the application of mathematical functions to describe policy performance trajectories. The reviewer distinguishes between policy outcomes that exhibit "logarithmic" (对数函数性) characteristics and those that exhibit "exponential" (指数函数性) characteristics. This metaphor provides a powerful diagnostic tool for evaluating the efficacy of Chinese governance reforms.

In bibliometric, spatial, and economic modeling literature, exponential and logarithmic functions are frequently utilized to map growth and saturation trends. For example, studies tracking the issuance of government policies against COVID-19 or the growth of urban-rural demographics often find that early, explosive growth fits an exponential curve, while maturing systems encountering structural limits fit a logarithmic or logistic curve.

### The Logarithmic Trap: Diminishing Returns in Governance

A logarithmic function, expressed broadly as $y = a \ln(x) + b$, is characterized by a steep initial slope that gradually flattens out as $x$ increases, eventually approaching an asymptote. The reviewer argues that campaign-style governance and administrative contracting structurally produce logarithmic outcomes.

Consider targeted poverty alleviation. When the central government issues a paramount political mandate to eradicate absolute poverty, local governments mobilize massive financial transfers, assign cadres directly to villages, and utilize all informal networks to achieve the target. The initial results are visually staggering—millions lifted over the poverty line in a short period. This is the steep initial slope of the logarithmic curve.

However, as the campaign progresses, the marginal cost of preventing those populations from sliding back into poverty increases dramatically. Because the rapid success was achieved through ad-hoc resource dumping rather than the establishment of a universal, sustainable social welfare institution, the governance capacity reaches a plateau. The structural vulnerabilities remain unaddressed. Environmental cleanups follow the same trajectory: shutting down highly polluting factories yields immediate, dramatic improvements in air quality, but transitioning the entire regional economy toward sustainable, high-tech manufacturing proves exceptionally difficult, resulting in a flattening curve of progress.

### The Exponential Imperative: Compounding Institutional Dividends

In contrast, an exponential function, expressed as $y = a e^{kx}$, begins with a remarkably slow, almost imperceptible rate of growth. In the early stages, the output appears negligible compared to the input. However, once the curve reaches an inflection point, growth compounds rapidly and sustainably.

The reviewer argues that true state capacity building must follow an exponential trajectory. The prime historical example provided is the 1994 Tax-Sharing Reform (分税制改革). At its inception, the reform was fraught with intense political friction. It required the central government to claw back fiscal authority from deeply entrenched local interests. The immediate, short-term governance outputs were highly disruptive, and local governments faced severe budgetary shortfalls. This represents the flat, early stage of the exponential curve.

However, the long-term institutional dividends were massive. The reform fundamentally rationalized the national macroeconomic structure, established a modern tax administration system, and provided the central state with the fiscal capacity necessary to steer national development over the subsequent decades. The success was durable, compounding, and deeply institutionalized.

### Table 2: Mathematical Paradigms of Policy Implementation

|**Dimension**|**Logarithmic Governance (y=aln(x))**|**Exponential Governance (y=aekx)**|
|---|---|---|
|**Primary Mechanism**|Campaign-style mobilization, ACS, resource dumping.|Institutional reform, rule of law, centralization of core public goods.|
|**Initial Phase**|Rapid, highly visible success; high political returns.|High political friction, resistance, slow visible progress.|
|**Long-term Phase**|Stagnation, diminishing returns, high risk of regression.|Compounding dividends, systemic stability, sustainable growth.|
|**Example Domains**|Targeted poverty alleviation, River Chief system, short-term GDP targets.|1994 Tax-Sharing Reform, universal healthcare systems, unified national markets.|
|**State Power Type**|Despotic power exercised through local agents.|True infrastructural power embedded in society.|

The reviewer's assertion that future reforms must seek "exponential" experiences is a profound indictment of the ACS framework. It implies that relying on the administrative contracting system to navigate China's transition into a high-income, technologically advanced economy is mathematically and institutionally flawed. The easy gains—the logarithmic spikes—have already been harvested. The nation is now operating in what the reviewer terms the "deep water zone" (深水区), where the remaining challenges are structural and point directly at the central government's institutional architecture.

## Future Trajectories: Central Expansion and the Restructuring of Public Goods

The culmination of the reviewer's analysis points toward a necessary paradigm shift in Chinese statecraft. The traditional "logic of empire"—characterized by a small, centralized decision-making core that subcontracts the massive burden of governance to local authorities —is reaching its historical and economic limits.

The reviewer boldly prescribes a future of "Central Expansion, Local Retreat" (中央要扩张，地方要退缩). This does not merely mean a centralization of political power, which has already been largely achieved; rather, it demands a centralization of _governance responsibility and fiscal burden_.

Currently, the central government carries "light baggage" while demanding heavy lifting from local authorities. National public goods with massive spillover effects—such as basic scientific research, cross-regional environmental protection, national social security nets, and advanced technological industrialization—cannot be efficiently managed through administrative contracting. Local governments, constrained by their geographic jurisdictions and evaluated on short-term metrics, lack both the incentive and the macroeconomic perspective to manage these exponential challenges. As noted in studies of technological capacity, a large economy trying to achieve industrialization requires indigenous technological capacities, supported by robust state capacity and leadership, rather than fragmented local competition.

If China is to avoid the middle-income trap and develop indigenous technological capacities, the central state must transition from a principal that merely monitors agents to an active provider of public goods. This requires a massive expansion of the central government's direct administrative apparatus and a corresponding reduction in the unfunded mandates imposed on local governments.

Accompanying this expansion must be a fundamental reform of the "attention mechanism" at the center. In a highly centralized system, the limited attention of top leadership becomes a severe bottleneck. The administrative contracting system previously bypassed this bottleneck by decentralizing execution. If the center is to expand its direct infrastructural power, it must institutionalize decision-making and diversify its administrative bandwidth, which inherently calls for systemic political and institutional modernization.

## Conclusion

The blog post provides a vital corrective to the prevailing academic consensus surrounding the Administrative Contracting System. By meticulously deconstructing Zhou Li-An's theoretical mapping, the reviewer exposes the conceptual stretching inherent in applying Michael Mann's "infrastructural power" to intra-bureaucratic delegation. The critique correctly identifies that "basic capacity" is a matter of political institutionalization, not merely information technology. Furthermore, the identification of the logarithmic nature of campaign-style governance versus the exponential necessity of structural reform provides a highly sophisticated analytical lens for viewing China's future policy trajectories.

Ultimately, the reviewer demonstrates that while the Administrative Contracting System may eloquently describe the mechanics of China's past economic mobilization, it is wholly insufficient as a theoretical blueprint for building the true, embedded infrastructural power required for the nation's next stage of historical development. The transition from an administrative contractor to a modern, public-service-oriented state remains the paramount challenge of Chinese governance.

# 对“行政发包制”的分析性评估：中国治理中国家能力范式的理论与实践批判

## 引言：充满争议的国家能力范式

长期以来，关于中国国家能力及其独特官僚机制的学术讨论，一直被试图将西方理论框架与中国治理结构的真实历史和政治现实相调和的努力所主导。在这一持续的学术探索中，一个突出的框架是“行政发包制”（Administrative Contracting System, ACS），该理论由周黎安等学者进行了广泛的理论化阐述 。该框架认为，中国国家机器的运作方式是韦伯式官僚制与企业外包模式的混合体，中央政府作为委托人，将巨大的经济、社会和政治责任下放给地方政府这些代理人 。虽然这一理论为中国经济的快速崛起提供了强大的解释力——特别是当它与政治晋升锦标赛的动态机制相结合时 ——但它也引来了严格的批判性审视。

本文所评估的博客文章对行政发包制框架提出了深刻且极具分析性的批评，特别挑战了其2022年的迭代版本，该版本试图将行政发包机制映射到更广泛的国家能力理论文献中 。评论者的核心论点是，行政发包制框架从根本上误用了成熟的西方国家能力概念——即迈克尔·曼（Michael Mann）关于“专制性权力”（despotic power）与“基础性权力”（infrastructural power）的区分——以此来掩盖中国央地治理模式中的结构性缺陷。此外，评论者认为，“基础能力”的概念存在技术还原论的倾向，忽视了制度性权力的深层政治属性。最后，评论者运用了一个极具原创性的数学隐喻，对比了对数函数和指数函数型的政策结果，断言当前的官僚激励结构仅仅是为了实现短期的、运动式的成功而优化的，而不是为了中国未来稳定所需的长期、复利式的制度改革。

本报告对评论者的批评进行了详尽、多维度的评估。通过综合大流行病控制、粮食安全、环境治理和房地产等各个政策领域的实证数据，并将这些数据置于历史和社会学框架中进行语境化分析，本分析旨在揭示行政发包制在认识论和实践层面的局限性。

## 基础性权力在认识论上的误用

评论者提出的基础性批评集中在对迈克尔·曼被广泛引用的国家权力类型学的误用上。曼（1984年）著名地将国家权力分为两个截然不同的类别：“专制性权力”和“基础性权力” 。专制性权力指的是国家精英在无需与公民社会团体进行常规、制度化协商的情况下所能采取的行动范围 。它是中央权威自主行动的权力，必要时具有强制性。相反，基础性权力被定义为国家渗透公民社会并在整个国家疆域内无缝贯彻政治决定的后勤保障能力 。这一概念在比较政治学中产生了深远影响，被用来解释从拉丁美洲到东亚国家有效性的差异 。

在行政发包制框架中，委托人（中央政府或上级权威）与代理人（地方政府或第三方承包商）之间的动态关系在结构上被映射到了这些概念上。中央政府分配任务和否决结果的能力等同于专制性权力，而地方政府对资源的实际控制、执行和自由裁量权则被构建为基础性权力 。

评论者敏锐地指出这是一个关键的概念错误。基础性权力，在其最初的社会学概念中，描述的是 _国家与社会_ 之间的关系，而不是 _国家机器不同层级_ 之间的关系。当一个县级政府执行来自北京的指令时，地方政府并没有充当社会的“基础设施”；它仅仅是国家官僚等级制度中的一个从属节点，行使着被授予的行政权力。

### 表 1：国家权力概念化中的理论分歧

|**分析维度**|**迈克尔·曼的原始框架**|**行政发包制框架**|**评论者的批评**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**权力的核心作用点**|国家机构与公民社会之间的连结。|上下级政府之间的等级关系。|将官僚体制内的权力下放与真正的国家渗透社会混为一谈。|
|**专制性权力**|国家无需与社会协商即可行动的能力。|中央权威分配任务、监督和否决的权力。|中央对地方代理人的控制是行政等级制，而非对社会的专制权力。|
|**基础性权力**|对社会的后勤渗透（税收、治安、公共服务）。|地方政府的自由裁量执行和资源动员能力。|错误地将地方行政执行权（事权）认作基础性权力。|

通过将地方行政执行权重新贴上“基础性权力”的标签，行政发包制框架掩盖了乔尔·米格代尔（Joel Migdal）所提出的“强社会，弱国家”范式的现实 。正如评论者所指出的，真正的基础性权力需要一个统一的、专业化的、深深嵌入民众之中并按照制度化规则行事的国家机器 。当中央国家将其义务外包给地方代理人时，通常依赖代理人的非正式网络、人际关系和强制手段来实现目标，这本质上与韦伯式国家中以规则为约束的、理性化渗透的高基础性权力背道而驰。

## “基础能力”的谬误与技术决定论

评论者批评的第二个主要支柱针对的是行政发包制框架中使用的“基础能力”概念。在发包模式中，基础能力被呈现为发包方和承包方可用的治理技术、专业知识和资源动员能力的综合体。它被框架化为决定专制权威能否成功转化为基础性成果的关键变量。

评论者对此提出挑战，认为行政发包制框架将国家能力的演进简化为信息技术和数据处理能力的单纯进步。行政发包制框架隐含的逻辑是，如果通过更好的监控技术解决央地之间的信息不对称问题，委托代理问题就会得到缓解，从而带来最佳的治理结果。

这种技术决定论从根本上无视了国家形成的历史现实。正如评论者正确指出的那样，西欧国家在现代信息技术出现之前很久，就已经建立起稳健的韦伯式官僚系统和强大的基础性权力 。这些早期现代国家的成功并非建立在数字监控或实时数据传输的基础上，而是建立在政治和制度选择上：政治与行政的严格分离、非人格化的法治的建立以及财政收入的集中化。

评论者认为，“基础能力”不是一个信息技术问题，而是一个关于央地政治动态的深刻问题。在某些领域未能实现高度的基础性权力，并非因为缺乏数据，而是因为一种深思熟虑的政治策略——“帝国逻辑” ——即中央国家有意维持一种灵活、模糊、分权的行政结构，以尽量减少其直接治理成本并转移地方的不满情绪。

考察中国国家治理的先秦起源，这种历史连续性显而易见。春秋战国时期郡县设立的数据表明，早期国家的形成在很大程度上依赖于军事动员和贵族宗族对资源的直接榨取，而不是一个集中的、统一的后勤官僚机构 。行政发包制的现代迭代用市县政府取代了贵族封地，但潜在的逻辑依然存在：中央要求结果，却将后勤和财政负担向下转移。因此，用“治理技术”取代真正的政治制度化，是在为中国官僚模式长期存在的脆弱性进行合理化辩护，而不是为克服这些脆弱性提供路线图。

## 发包制的机制与扭曲

为了充分理解评论者批评的深度，有必要审查行政发包制的三个功能维度：行政权力的分配、预算分配以及绩效考核机制 。评论者认为，将这三个维度与国家能力的核心原则（决策自主性、资源动员和政策执行）对齐，是一种牵强附会的映射。

### 权力分配与虚假的自主性

在纯粹的韦伯式官僚制中，代理人受到严格的程序规则的约束，旨在消除任意的自由裁量权。在行政发包制中，中央权威保留了名义上的控制权，但巨大的自由裁量权在没有正式法律授权的情况下被转移给了地方官员 。这种结构上的模糊性迫使地方官员依赖非正式的、甚至通常是法外的机制来完成指令。行政发包制所赞颂的自主性，实际上是地方代理人在巨大的自上而下的压力下运作的一种生存机制，而非发展型国家所特有的真正“嵌入式自主性”。

### 预算分配与财政联邦制

也许评论者指出的最严重的扭曲是在资源汲取领域。行政发包制的特点是中央政府对收入的集中汲取，同时伴随着支出负担的地方化。与西方国家由国家税务机构直接为国家倡议提供资金不同，中国的地方政府被迫为中央的指令自筹资金 。

这种动态具有深远的宏观经济后果。这是中国依赖土地财政和地方政府融资平台（LGFV）扩散的主要驱动力 。因为地方政府缺乏满足其承包目标的正式财政能力，它们将土地商品化并利用债务杠杆，在房地产市场制造了系统性的金融风险 。评论者正确地指出，将这种对预算外收入的绝望追求框架化为一种“资源汲取和动员能力”是极具问题的。它以企业家式治理为幌子，掩盖了严重功能失调的财政安排。

### 绩效考核与错位的激励

行政发包制的第三个维度涉及以结果为导向的、高度人格化的绩效考核，通常被描述为政治锦标赛 。对地方官员的评估不是基于他们对法治的遵守，而是基于他们完成可量化目标（如GDP增长）的能力 。

实证文献强烈支持评论者的断言，即这会导致严重的激励错位。例如，在农业政策领域，粮食安全责任（FSR）制度将国家粮食安全的重担转移给地方政府。因为地方官员主要受到GDP增长和晋升的激励，他们将农业生产视为一种高成本、低回报的努力 。因此，他们战略性地调整自己的行为，加剧如过度灌溉等不可持续的做法，以牺牲长期生态稳定为代价来满足短期的粮食配额 。同样，在COVID-19大流行期间，地方封锁政策的差异表明，地方官员优先考虑与北京目标挂钩的职业前景，通常导致过度合规（严厉的封锁）或根据地方能力限制进行选择性执行，这反映了一个受政治意图而非客观基础能力驱动的系统 。

## 运动式治理与可持续制度化

博客评论者特别反对行政发包制框架将“政治锦标赛”作为国家能力的最终引擎。发包制文献认为，强晋升激励和强行政发包的结合会导致高效的治理。相反，晋升激励弱且发包弱的领域被认为是低效的。

评论者挑战了这一矩阵的有效性。他们观察到，以“晋升激励弱、行政发包弱”为特征的领域——如国家航天计划或高铁发展——往往非常成功，恰恰是因为它们脱离了地方发包系统，由中央国家直接管理。这一观察对行政发包制范式造成了重大打击。它表明，当国家需要真正的、不打折扣的基础性权力时，它会完全绕过行政发包系统，回归直接的、集中的官僚管理。

此外，评论者强调了“运动式治理”的局限性，这是行政发包制的自然副产品。当地方政府面临来自上级的解决特定问题（如精准扶贫或环境整治）的强烈、周期性压力时，他们会在短暂的时间内动员所有可用的正式和非正式资源。虽然这可以产生惊人的短期结果，但它未能建立长期治理所需的持久的、基于规则的制度。

“河长制”的实施经常被作为地方环境治理政策创新的胜利被引用 ，但评论者批评这仅仅是“补课”。将快速的环境修复视为国家能力的突破，忽略了一个事实，即环境退化正是由于几十年来优先考虑GDP而非生态的相同的行政发包系统所造成的 。

## 治理的数学原理：对数函数与指数函数范式

评论者批评中最深刻、最具概念创新性的一点是应用数学函数来描述政策绩效轨迹。评论者区分了表现出“对数函数性”特征的政策结果和表现出“指数函数性”特征的政策结果。这个隐喻为评估中国治理改革的效能提供了一个强大的诊断工具。

在文献计量学、空间学和经济建模文献中，指数和对数函数经常被用来描绘增长和饱和趋势 。例如，跟踪针对COVID-19的政府政策发布或城乡人口增长的研究通常发现，早期的爆炸性增长符合指数曲线，而遇到结构性限制的成熟系统则符合对数或逻辑斯谛曲线 。

### 对数陷阱：治理中的边际收益递减

对数函数，大致表示为 $y = a \ln(x) + b$，其特征是初始斜率陡峭，随着 $x$ 的增加逐渐趋于平缓，最终接近渐近线。评论者认为，运动式治理和行政发包制在结构上产生对数结果。

以精准扶贫为例。当中央政府下达消除绝对贫困的最高政治指令时，地方政府调动大量财政转移支付，直接派干部驻村，并利用一切非正式网络来实现目标。最初的结果在视觉上是惊人的——数百万人在短时间内脱贫。这就是对数曲线陡峭的初始斜率。

然而，随着运动的推进，防止这些人群返贫的边际成本急剧增加。因为快速的成功是通过临时性的资源倾注实现的，而不是建立普遍的、可持续的社会福利制度，治理能力达到了一个平台期。结构性脆弱性依然未被解决。环境清理遵循同样的轨迹：关闭高污染工厂可以在空气质量上产生直接、显著的改善，但将整个区域经济向可持续、高科技制造业转型却异常困难，导致进展曲线趋于平缓。

### 指数性必然：制度红利的复利效应

相反，指数函数（表示为 $y = a e^{kx}$）从一个非常缓慢、几乎察觉不到的增长率开始。在早期阶段，与投入相比，产出似乎微不足道。然而，一旦曲线达到拐点，增长就会迅速且可持续地产生复利。

评论者认为，真正的国家能力建设必须遵循指数轨迹。提供的主要历史例子是1994年的分税制改革。在改革初期，充满了激烈的政治摩擦。它要求中央政府从根深蒂固的地方利益中收回财政权力。直接的、短期的治理产出具有高度破坏性，地方政府面临严重的预算短缺。这代表了指数曲线平坦的早期阶段。

然而，长期的制度红利是巨大的。该改革从根本上理顺了国家宏观经济结构，建立了现代税收征管体系，并为中央国家提供了在随后几十年引导国家发展所需的财政能力 。这种成功是持久的、具有复利效应的，并且是深度制度化的。

### 表 2：政策执行的数学范式

|**维度**|**对数型治理 (y=aln(x))**|**指数型治理 (y=aekx)**|
|---|---|---|
|**主要机制**|运动式动员、行政发包制、资源倾注。|制度改革、法治、核心公共产品中央化。|
|**初始阶段**|快速、高度可见的成功；高政治回报。|高政治摩擦、阻力、进展缓慢且不明显。|
|**长期阶段**|停滞、边际收益递减、返贫/倒退风险高。|复利红利、系统稳定性、可持续增长。|
|**领域示例**|精准扶贫、河长制、短期GDP目标。|1994年分税制改革、全民医疗体系、全国统一大市场。|
|**国家权力类型**|通过地方代理人行使的专制性权力。|真正嵌入社会的基础性权力。|

评论者断言未来的改革必须寻找“指数函数性”经验，这是对行政发包制框架的深刻控诉。它意味着，依靠行政发包制来引导中国向高收入、技术先进的经济体转型，在数学和制度上都是有缺陷的。容易获得的成果——对数式的爆发——已经被收割完毕。国家现在正运行在评论者所称的“深水区”，剩下的挑战是结构性的，直指中央政府的制度架构。

## 未来轨迹：中央扩张与公共产品的重构

评论者分析的高潮指向了中国国家治理模式必须发生的范式转变。传统的“帝国逻辑”——特征是一个小型的、集中的决策核心，将庞大的治理重担外包给地方当局 ——正在达到其历史和经济极限。

评论者大胆地提出了“中央要扩张，地方要退缩”的未来处方。这不仅仅意味着政治权力的集中，这在很大程度上已经实现；相反，它要求 _治理责任和财政负担_ 的集中。

目前，中央政府“挑轻担子”，却要求地方当局承担重任。具有巨大外溢效应的全国性公共产品——如基础科学研究、全国社会保障网和先进技术产业化——无法通过行政发包进行有效管理。受限于地理管辖权并根据短期指标进行评估的地方政府，既缺乏动机也缺乏宏观经济视角来管理这些指数级的挑战 。正如关于技术能力的研究所指出的，一个试图实现工业化的大型经济体需要本土技术能力，这需要中央国家能力和领导力的支持，而不是零碎的地方竞争 。

如果中国要避免中等收入陷阱并发展本土技术能力，中央国家必须从仅仅监控代理人的委托人，转变为公共产品的积极提供者。这要求大规模扩张中央政府的直接行政机构，并相应减少强加给地方政府的无资金支持的指令。

伴随这种扩张的，必须是中央“注意力机制”的根本改革。在一个高度集权的系统中，最高领导层的有限注意力成为一个严重的瓶颈。行政发包制以前通过分散执行绕过了这个瓶颈。如果中央要扩展其直接的基础性权力，它必须将决策制度化并使其行政带宽多样化，这内在地呼唤着系统性的政治和制度现代化。

## 结论

这篇博客文章对围绕行政发包制的流行学术共识提供了至关重要的纠正。通过对周黎安理论映射的细致解构，评论者揭露了将迈克尔·曼的“基础性权力”应用于官僚体制内部授权所固有的概念过度延伸。该批评准确地指出，“基础能力”是一个政治制度化的问题，而不仅仅是信息技术问题。此外，对运动式治理的对数性质与结构性改革的指数必然性的识别，为观察中国未来的政策轨迹提供了一个高度复杂的分析视角。

归根结底，评论者证明，虽然行政发包制可能雄辩地描述了中国过去经济动员的机制，但作为构建国家下一个历史发展阶段所需的真正、嵌入的基础性权力的理论蓝图，它是完全不充分的。从一个行政发包方转变为一个现代的、以公共服务为导向的国家，仍然是中国治理面临的最高挑战。
